# How highly cited DHS and MICS papers are written

Corpus: 595 open access PubMed papers that name DHS or MICS in the title or abstract and have more than 50 citations (NIH iCite, October 2026). Analyses below use the 579 original research articles. 97 of them appeared in top general or global health journals (Lancet family, BMJ family, PLOS Medicine, JAMA, NEJM, Bulletin of the WHO, IJE).

Citations: median 72 (IQR 59 to 97; n=579). Publication years: 2003 to 2023.

Most common journals: PloS one (98); BMC public health (83); BMC pregnancy and childbirth (35); BMJ open (27); PLoS medicine (26); Reproductive health (21); BMC women's health (17); International journal for equity in health (16); BMC health services research (14); BMC pediatrics (14); The Lancet. Global health (14); BMJ global health (13).

## Titles
* Length: median 16 (IQR 13 to 19; n=579) words.
* Contains a colon (two-part title): 66%
* Names the country or region: 89%
* Names the data source (DHS, MICS, survey): 32%
* Names the design or method (cross-sectional, multilevel, analysis): 39%
* Phrased as a question: 5%
* Uses 'evidence from': 12%
* Uses 'factors associated' / 'determinants': 32%

## Abstract
* Structured abstract: 85% of papers.
* Most common heading sets: Background / Methods / Results / Conclusions (136); Background / Methods / Results / Conclusion (130); Background / Methods and findings / Conclusions (25); Background / Methods / Findings / Interpretation / Funding (17); Introduction / Methods / Results / Conclusion (15); Background / Methods / Result / Conclusion (10).
* Total length: median 313 (IQR 271 to 359; n=571) words.
* Background part: median 58 (IQR 43 to 73; n=511) words.
* Methods part: median 73 (IQR 55 to 98; n=453) words.
* Results part: median 124 (IQR 94 to 158; n=456) words.
* Conclusion part: median 54 (IQR 41 to 72; n=485) words.
* Abstract gives sample size: 39%
* Abstract names survey year(s): 82%
* Abstract reports an effect estimate (OR/RR/HR/PR/coefficient): 28%
* Abstract reports 95% CI: 43%
* Abstract reports p values: 12%
* Abstract reports percentages: 78%
* Abstract conclusion contains a policy or action word: 78% of structured abstracts

## Overall structure and length
* Most common top-level section orders: introduction > methods > results > discussion > conclusion (379); introduction > methods > results > discussion (142); introduction > methods > results > discussion > limitations > conclusion (13); introduction > methods > results > conclusion (10); methods > results > discussion (7).
* Introduction: median 622 (IQR 493 to 809; n=561) words; median 5 (IQR 4 to 6; n=561) paragraphs.
* Methods: median 989 (IQR 752 to 1296; n=568) words; median 8 (IQR 6 to 11; n=568) paragraphs.
* Results: median 1140 (IQR 737 to 1794; n=568) words; median 6 (IQR 5 to 9; n=568) paragraphs.
* Discussion: median 1155 (IQR 897 to 1488; n=561) words; median 8 (IQR 6 to 10; n=561) paragraphs.
* Conclusion: median 134 (IQR 100 to 197; n=416) words; median 1 (IQR 1 to 1; n=416) paragraphs.
* Separate Conclusion section: 72%. Limitations under their own heading or subheading: 30%.

## Introduction
* States burden with numbers (%, rates, deaths): 88%
* Cites global targets (SDG, MDG, WHO, UN targets): 40%
* Describes evidence gap ('little is known', 'few studies'): 55%
* Ends with explicit aim/objective (in the last paragraph): 65%
* Uses a theory or conceptual framework: 18%
* Names the country context: 99%
