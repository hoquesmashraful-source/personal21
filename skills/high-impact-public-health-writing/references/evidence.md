# Why the most cited public health articles are cited

Top 100: the most cited public health articles published 2000 to 2024 (NIH iCite citation counts, October 2026; definition in scripts/build_top_cited.py). Controls: 163 articles with an abstract, drawn at random from the same journal and year (137 items without an abstract, mostly news and commentary, were dropped).

* Citations, top 100: median 4496 (range 3122 to 15076). Controls: median 171.
* Relative citation ratio, top 100: median 182 (1 = NIH median).
* Publication period: 2000 to 2004: 20; 2005 to 2009: 17; 2010 to 2014: 25; 2015 to 2019: 23; 2020 to 2024: 15.
* Journals: Lancet (52); JAMA (16); N Engl J Med (8); Am J Epidemiol (6); Int J Epidemiol (4); PLoS Med (2); Lancet Public Health (2); BMC Public Health (2); Lancet Glob Health (2); BMJ (2); Epidemiology (2); Bull World Health Organ (1).
