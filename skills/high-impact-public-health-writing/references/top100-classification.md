# The 100 most cited public health articles, 2000 to 2024

Ranked by NIH iCite citations (October 2026). Each paper is coded by its
primary contribution and by what citing authors most likely reuse (manual,
single-coder judgement). Eight papers without an abstract were coded from
title, authors and article type. Use this list to find exemplars: look up a
paper by PMID when you want to see how a top paper handled a section.

## Categories

* **Estimates:** levels, trends or attributable burden for a country, region
  or the world.
* **Method:** a new or clarified analytic approach, checklist or rule.
* **Standard:** a cut-off, growth reference or index applied in measurement.
* **Framework:** a named construct, definition or organising model.
* **Finding:** a landmark association.
* **Outbreak:** first data on an emerging threat.
* **Synthesis:** review, meta-analysis, Lancet Series paper or Commission.
* **Data resource:** a description or validation of a dataset others use.

Reuse: **Number** (an Introduction figure), **Method or data** (a Methods
citation), **Standard**, **Framework**, **Comparator** (a finding to compare
against).
